This project will use MassCourtsPlus court records to identify and analyze medical-debt lawsuits in Massachusetts. Because cases aren’t labeled “medical,” we will reconstruct the medical signal from party names and docket/action text, supported by entity lists (providers, ambulance services, medical-credit issuers, debt buyers) and keyword rules. We will use SQL and Python to implement classification and ensure results are transparent and reproducible.

Our intended datasets are: (1) MassCourtsPlus tables for cases, parties, actions/dockets, court types, locations, and outcomes; (2) prior DS701 cleaned plaintiff/name-normalization files and focused subsets (e.g., CareCredit/Synchrony, ambulance cases); (3) MA Division of Banks Debt Collector Annual Reports (2022–2024) to identify collectors linked to medical debt; (4) optional demographic overlays to explore disparities; and (5) relevant public reporting (e.g., WBUR, KFF Health News) for context.

Our objectives are to produce a defensible minimum estimate of how many consumer-debt lawsuits are truly medical, identify the top filers, show where and in which courts they sue (small claims, civil, superior), summarize defendant outcomes, and deliver a transparent, reproducible pipeline with publication-ready visuals for GBH’s investigative reporting.
